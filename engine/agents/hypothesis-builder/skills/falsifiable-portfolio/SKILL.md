# Falsifiable hypothesis portfolio

A useful hypothesis predicts a security-relevant observation that differs from a
benign control. “Could be vulnerable” is not a hypothesis. Name the causal
mechanism, attacker-controlled variable, required state, boundary crossed and
effect. Then state the cleanest observation that would prove the mechanism wrong.

Keep at least one alternative from a different mechanism or boundary so the
campaign does not collapse around its first attractive explanation.
